# Google Maps Scraper Kit

This skill guides evaluating and managing Google Maps lead extraction pipelines, technical limits, and contact field enrichment.

Refer directly to the operational guide in [google-maps-scraper](../google-maps-scraper/SKILL.md).

## Highlights:
- **Available Fields:** Business Name, Category/Type, Address, Phone Number, Website, Latitude/Longitude, Rating, Total Reviews, Place ID, Google Maps URL, Status.
- **Enrichment:** Website scanning for corporate emails, WhatsApp direct link generation (+55 DDD), social profile discovery.
- **Technical Limits:** Paginated depth control (`--depth`), query concurrency limits, rate limiting delays to prevent IP bans.
- **Compliance:** Use authorized search terms and avoid harvesting personal data directly from social media.
